Helpful Information

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before checking into Spillway Private Pool Villa.

What are the standard Check-in and Check-out timings?

Standard Check-in is at 2:00 PM and Check-out is by 11:00 AM. Early check-in or late check-out can often be accommodated upon prior request depending on booking schedules.

Is the swimming pool entirely private to our group?

Yes, 100%! When you book Spillway Private Pool Villa, the swimming pool and entire villa grounds are exclusively reserved for your booked party. There are no other guests on the property.

How many guests can the villa accommodate?

With 6 spacious bedrooms and 5 bathrooms, the villa comfortably accommodates 12 to 18+ guests with extra mattress options available upon request.

Can we arrange campfire and barbecue?

Yes, we have a dedicated outdoor bonfire pit and grill facility. Our resident caretaker can help arrange wood, charcoal, and setup on advance notice.

Are food arrangements or self-cooking options available?

The villa features an equipped kitchen with utensils and stove for light cooking. Additionally, delicious traditional Kerala home-cooked meals can be arranged on pre-order through nearby home chefs or caretakers.
